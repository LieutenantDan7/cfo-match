# CFO Match — Pilot Pricing

## Launch objective
Get the first provider partners live quickly, validate lead quality, and close first revenue without overcomplicating the commercial model.

## Pilot model
Start with a simple beta structure for the first 30-60 days.

### Option A — Founding partner beta
- $250/month founding partner placement
- Includes:
  - provider listing/profile in beta cohort
  - access to matched lead opportunities
  - early input into routing rules
  - preferred consideration during the pilot

### Option B — Pay per qualified lead
- $150 per qualified lead
- Qualified lead means:
  - business contact submitted complete intake
  - service need clearly matches fractional CFO / outsourced finance
  - timeline is ASAP / this month / next 90 days
  - company context is sufficient to route

## Recommendation
Use this sequence:
1. Offer **first 2-3 providers** the founding partner beta at **$250/month**
2. If they resist recurring pricing, offer **$150/qualified lead** as fallback
3. After the first wins and data, raise to:
   - $500/month founding placement
   - or $200-$300/qualified lead depending on fit and segment

## Positioning
This is not a high-volume spam lead source.
The value proposition is:
- tighter qualification
- smaller distribution per lead
- better-fit buyer intent
- faster access to relevant opportunities

## Provider talking points
- small beta cohort
- quality over volume
- limited competition per lead
- faster buyer/provider matching
- chance to shape the channel early

## Commercial guardrails
- no long-term commitment during beta
- cancel anytime during first 30 days
- replace obviously invalid leads in the pay-per-lead model
- keep routing narrow rather than maximizing lead resale
